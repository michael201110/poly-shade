# PolyShade 0.2.8 car contact shadow

Sun shadow maps combine occluders into one visibility mask. When a roof blocks the sun, the car cannot add a second shadow into that already-occluded region. PolyShade now draws a small soft contact shadow beneath the nearest opaque car, independent of direct sunlight.

The shadow ray-tests only classified road, grass and concrete meshes at 8 Hz and reuses one transparent circle mesh. It aligns to the hit surface, excludes the car and replay ghosts, and is removed on scene detach. Golden Hour Lite, Golden Hour and Capture enable it by default; Vanilla leaves it off. The **Car contact shadow** option controls it.
