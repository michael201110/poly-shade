# PolyShade 0.2.4 ray quality and performance

PolyShade 0.2.4 improves the visible ray sampling and skips expensive sun-shaft passes when the sun is fully visible or fully blocked.

## Changes

Radial rays now use 48 depth-mask taps instead of 32, with a broader 3×3 final filter to soften remaining sampling edges. Ray and volumetric targets remain half resolution. Both effects share the same visibility probe and now render only when that probe reports partial sun occlusion; open-sky frames avoid those GPU passes. Golden Hour Capture now renders at display resolution with FXAA and 2048 shadows instead of 1.25× supersampling and 4096 shadows, reducing frame cost on a 720p canvas.

## Verification

The unit suite checks that partially occluded sunlight enables both shaft passes and a fully visible sun skips them. Live PML verification exercises arch occlusion, reports pass timing and confirms ray and volume target sizes. On the verification machine, Capture GPU p95 fell from about 24 ms to 12 ms and CPU p95 from 22 ms to 9 ms after removing supersampling and reducing shadow maps. This is one machine's measurement, not a guarantee for every GPU. Results are written to `%TEMP%/polyshade-0.2.4/`.
