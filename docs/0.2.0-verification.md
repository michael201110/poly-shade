# PolyShade 0.2.0 verification

Verified 7 October 2026 against the actual official PML CDN PolyTrack 0.6.3 game, using headless Microsoft Edge on this machine. This is a measured development verification, not a universal performance benchmark.

## Base and release

- Starting commit: `819e59dcf03365f8a249b96cb0c833e2db4dfc7a` (0.1.2).
- New release: 0.2.0; root manifest target: PolyTrack 0.6.3.
- `0.1.0/`, `0.1.1/` and `0.1.2/` verified unchanged against the starting commit.
- Generated ESM bundle: 128869 bytes; PML PolyTypes is the only external module import.

## Runtime discovery

WebGL2, HDR half-float render targets, depth textures, 4x scene MSAA, Standard/Physical PBR materials, PointLight, ShaderMaterial, Raycaster and asynchronous GPU timer queries were available. Texture/renderbuffer limit: 16384; supported sample limit: 8.

The export registry also found scene/light/math/geometry/camera/texture/render-target primitives. It did not find CubeCamera, PMREMGenerator, MeshPhongMaterial, AmbientLight, RawShaderMaterial, FogExp2 or Clock. Legacy RGBFormat/sRGBEncoding are absent. No npm Three.js code ships in the mod.

## Sky, environment and lighting

Live screenshots show broad sky gradients and the approved two-layer soft cloud forms. Clouds are default-on in Golden Hour and Capture, off in Lite. Sun direction is shared by the disc, cloud tint, environment, lighting, shadows, aerial perspective and screen-space shafts. The sky shader ignores camera translation and restores the native sky mesh/background on disable.

A generated 128x64 / 256x128 / 512x256 equirectangular HDR sky uses the bundled engine?s internal PMREM conversion (approximately 32 / 64 / 128 cube faces). It regenerates on relevant settings changes, not per frame or per camera move. A unit test checks its UV/sun orientation against the actual bundle convention. The automatic sun colour warms low elevations, with independently adjustable sky and ground fills.

Cars use the sky environment live. The Main paint shader stays intact and its environment intensity is controlled separately. Inspected Main material: Standard, roughness ~0.4, environment intensity 0.6; named metal: roughness 0.3, metalness 0.75, environment intensity 0.66.

## Brake-light emission

Two actual red PointLights follow the native brake-lamp geometry and existing emissive on/off state. Live keyboard braking activated both lights and showed red illumination on the road and nearby barrier; release switched them off. Screenshots were inspected. Inactive lights are invisible to the renderer lighting calculation; active lamps are unshadowed, range-limited, and capped to the three nearest opaque cars. Ghosts are excluded, and disposal removes the lights. Controls/physics are never read or changed by the mod. Power/reach are adjustable. The real PointLight export was verified in the game bundle and live.

## Shadows and materials

Local shadows use adaptive, forward-biased, smoothed, quantized and texel-aligned camera coverage with 1024/2048/4096 maps and adjustable bias. Native CSM retains its original cascade layout and splits. Four cascades were verified live; Capture map sizes were [4096, 4096, 2048, 2048]. Distant cascades retain conservative native normal bias. No additional CSM sun is inserted.

Classification reports kind/confidence/evidence; explicit names take priority over corroborating geometry/colour and uncertain objects remain unknown. Green car paint is protected. Stable pattern overrides and Raycaster-based picking are available. Basic conversion prefers Standard and preserves normals/flat shading, maps, vertex colours, render flags and live source updates. Compatible matte materials receive subtle procedural roughness. Ghosts, translucent surfaces and arbitrary custom shader callbacks remain excluded.

## Post-processing and quality

Live passes succeeded: HDR scene/depth, reduced-resolution SSAO and bilateral blur, thresholded bloom and blur, depth-aware sun shafts, aerial perspective, linear grade, filmic tone mapping and final sRGB output. Capture sharpening is enabled. FXAA is implemented and optional, off in presets because native/scene MSAA is retained.

Scene colour is linear with no initial tone mapping; AO/bloom/shafts and grade operate before one filmic mapping; output is encoded once. Render state is captured/restored around the pipeline. Disabled passes do not run, their targets are released, and failed optional shaders are isolated.

Capture?s scene target was measured at 1600x900 for a 1280x720 canvas. Lite used two post targets, Golden Hour/Capture used seven while the sun was visible. Resize to 1024x640 and back was verified.

## UI, source and remaining limits

The grouped collapsible panel exposes sky, lighting/environment, shadows, material overrides/picking, atmosphere, grade/AO/bloom/shafts/AA/sharpening, quality and diagnostics. Serializable settings migrate schema 0/1 to schema 2.

Significant files: `src/rendering/{capabilities,cinematic,sky,environment,brake-lights,postprocess,render-targets,shader-guard,performance}.js`; `src/shaders/`; `src/options.js`; existing renderer/controller/effects/materials/settings/UI/presets/mod files; expanded `scripts/verify-live.mjs`; `test/cinematic.test.js`; metadata, README and generated 0.2.0 release.

The environment represents sky rather than nearby geometry. AO/shafts are screen-space effects and cannot see off-screen occluders; shafts intentionally reject foreground solids. Native CSM splits remain game-owned. Exported PMREM/CubeCamera absence requires the internal conversion route. No photorealistic HDRI, ray tracing or volumetric raymarching is used. PML manager-only unload may require a reload; explicit dispose/F7/Vanilla/page exit restore state.

Release commit/push/remote SHA/worktree verification are reported with the final delivery, after this report is committed.
