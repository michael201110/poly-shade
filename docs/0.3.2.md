# PolyShade 0.3.2

Adds depth-aware camera motion blur for the racing camera. For each pixel, the shader reconstructs its current world position from scene depth and projects it through the previous frame's camera. Four small samples blend along that screen path. Depth discontinuities reject taps across object edges, and the trail is capped in pixels. Camera cuts, camera switches, resize/projection changes and long frame gaps reset the previous view to suppress flashes.

The effect runs inside the existing colour pass and creates no render target. Four colour and up to four depth taps are added only when the pixel's camera motion exceeds half a pixel. The bounded defaults are subtle: Clear Day 0.14/7 px, Soft Overcast 0.12/7 px, Golden Hour 0.22/8 px and Capture 0.20/8 px. Vanilla and Golden Hour Lite leave it off. All values are adjustable under Post Processing.

This estimates camera motion from depth. It cannot blur a moving car independently while the camera is fixed because PolyTrack does not expose per-object velocity buffers to this mod. The shader blurs the scene before compositing sun rays, flare and volumetric shafts, which keeps those optical effects clear.
